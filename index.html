RainCast

Rain Tomorrow Predictor — logistic regression, trained on 1,000 synthetic weather days. All prediction happens in your browser; no data leaves this page.

Enter today's weather and press Predict rain tomorrow.

Model: 5-fold cross-validated accuracy = 76.5%, AUC = 0.85 (guessing the majority class: 50.6% — about 1.5× better). A chance above 50% is flagged as rain likely. Trained on synthetic data for learning purposes; real forecasts also use satellite data and numerical weather models.
